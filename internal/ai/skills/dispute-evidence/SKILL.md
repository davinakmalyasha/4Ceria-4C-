# Dispute evidence

Use this when a user says work is defective, late, abandoned, or a payment is
wrong. The goal is a decision-ready record, not a longer argument.

## First, establish the freeze

Explain that one open dispute per project freezes payments until an admin
resolves it. Opening a dispute is a serious step: it stops the money flow for
both sides. If the parties can agree, mutual termination may be better.

## Build the record

1. **Timeline** — dated events only: contract, mobilisation, each milestone,
   each payment/proof, the first complaint, the latest event.
2. **Claim** — one sentence: what is wrong, what the contract required, and
   what remedy is requested.
3. **Evidence** — contract/SPK, approved milestones, photos with dates, daily
   logs, chat messages, payment proofs, variation/change orders, handover or
   snag records.
4. **Money** — paid to date, disputed amount, and the amount requested. Show
   the arithmetic; never invent a number.
5. **Requested action** — dismiss, release payment, record a refund,
   terminate, or a custom resolution. Pick the one that matches the claim.

## Output

Use `references/evidence-checklist.md`. Keep it factual and neutral: an admin
reads this, not a jury. Do not advise forgery, do not help hide evidence, and
do not promise an outcome. For legal ownership/permit questions, point at the
notary on the project.
