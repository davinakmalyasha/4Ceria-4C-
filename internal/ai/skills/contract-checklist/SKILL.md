# Contract checklist

Use this before a client or professional signs, or when they ask "what should
I check in the SPK?".

## Check, in order

1. **Parties and project** — names, roles and the project title match the
   workspace. Flag a different legal entity.
2. **Scope and deliverables** — every deliverable in the brief has a line.
   Flag vague "as needed" language and missing exclusions.
3. **Price and tax** — the contract price equals the accepted bid; the tax
   treatment is stated. Never guess a tax rate.
4. **Payment termins** — percentages sum to 100%, each has a trigger, and
   the amounts equal the project price. Retention is 5% unless the contract
   says otherwise, and net + retention must equal the termin.
5. **Milestones** — each termin points at approved milestones. A termin with
   no linked milestone is a payment without a deliverable.
6. **Timeline** — start, duration, extension mechanism and delay remedies.
7. **Changes** — change orders are written and priced before work; no verbal
   extras.
8. **Warranty** — 180 days after BAST unless the contract is longer; snag
   items must be fixed before retention releases.
9. **Disputes** — the platform dispute route freezes payments while open;
   the contract should acknowledge it.
10. **Signatures** — professional signs first, then the client; both parties
    and dates are present.

## Output

Use `references/spk-checklist.md`, mark each item OK / FIX / MISSING, and list
only the material gaps. You review the record and the text the user provides;
you do not give legal advice and you do not sign anything.
